Ai Popup – How to delete your data

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Ai Popup keeps very little about people who comment on @aipop_up's posts: the username and Instagram-scoped ID needed to send one reply message to a comment. This is deleted automatically 40 days after the comment. See the privacy policy.

To have it deleted now

  1. Email gaurav11.pal@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data.
  2. Include your Instagram username.
  3. Your information is deleted within 7 days, and you get a reply confirming it.
