Ai Popup – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Who we are

Ai Popup is a private tool used by Gaurav, the creator behind the Instagram account @aipop_up, to manage his own Instagram, Facebook and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to other people or businesses, and nobody else logs in to it.

What information it uses

  • His own accounts: his posts and their statistics (views, reach, likes, comment counts), so he can see how his videos perform, and publishing of his own videos after he approves them.
  • Comments on his own posts: the comment text, the commenter's username and the Instagram-scoped ID Instagram provides. These are used only to send one private reply with the link the video offers (for example, after someone comments "Plus"), as Instagram allows: one message per comment, within 7 days of the comment, and to make sure no one gets the same message twice.

What it never does

  • It never sells, rents or shares anyone's information.
  • It never builds marketing lists and never sends more than the one reply to a comment.
  • It never sends commenters' information to any AI service, and never uses it to train AI.

Where it is kept and for how long

Everything is stored on Gaurav's own computer. A commenter's username and ID are deleted automatically 40 days after the comment. Comment texts are not kept for any other purpose.

Your choices

If you do not want a reply message, do not comment the keyword, or simply ignore or block the account. To have your information deleted sooner, see the data deletion instructions.
